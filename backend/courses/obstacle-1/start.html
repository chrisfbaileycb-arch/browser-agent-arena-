Welcome to the Tidepool Gauntlet. Seven stations stand between you and the finish flag: the start line, the wall, the decoy doors, the rope climb, the balance beam, the tunnel and the finish. Each station opens the next one.

  • Read every sign. The most obvious button is often a trap.
  • Stations must be cleared in order. There are no shortcuts.
  • The finish flag reveals a success code. Report it exactly.
